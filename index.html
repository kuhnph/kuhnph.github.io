About Me

To whom it may concern: my name is Philip Joseph Kuhn III. After eight semesters of engineering education, two years of internships, and more than a little hardship, I can call myself an engineer.

I cannot overstate how privileged I feel to be in this position. Some people think luck comes naturally, but I have always viewed it as the outcome of putting yourself in as many positions as possible to get lucky. I have now seen and done more than my younger self could have imagined, and I work as an aerospace engineer at the cutting edge of hypersonics.
